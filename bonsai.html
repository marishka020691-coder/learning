Frequently asked questions

What is included in each plan?

Workflow includes the features listed in the first card. Workflow Plus includes everything in Workflow, along with the extra features listed in the second card.

How do I switch between monthly and yearly billing?

Use the switch above the plans to change the billing period. Monthly prices are shown now; yearly prices will be added later.

Where can I explore the templates?

Open Templates in the navigation and choose a proposal, contract, or invoice to see a sample outline.

How do I choose a plan?

Select Start free under the plan you want to preview. This practice page shows your selection; it does not create an account or take a payment.
